Summary

AquariumAI collects no telemetry, requires no user account, and no registration. All your data (tanks, tasks, stock, photos, water parameters, dosing history) stays exclusively on your device. There is no server of ours in between.

What data goes where

There are exactly two situations in which the app talks to an external service at all — both strictly directly from your device, with no server of ours in between:

1. AI care profiles (optional feature, off by default)

If you enable this feature and fetch a care profile for a species, only the species name is sent to Anthropic's Claude API (api.anthropic.com). No tank, photo, location, or other user data is transmitted. The request uses your own, self-provided Anthropic API key (bring-your-own-key) — the app itself does not ship a key and does not earn anything from usage. Your API key is stored encrypted in the Android Keystore and leaves the device only to authenticate against the Claude API. How Anthropic processes these requests is governed by their own privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

2. Photo suggestions for stock entries (part of the same optional AI feature)

When searching for photos for a profile, the (usually scientific) species name is sent to the open Wikimedia Commons API (commons.wikimedia.org, meta.wikimedia.org) to suggest matching, license-free photos. No personal or usage data is transmitted — just the search term, like any regular web search.

What else stays local

  • Tank, task, stock, and water-parameter data in a local database on your device.
  • Photos you take or pick, stored in the app's internal storage.
  • An optional JSON backup export is stored wherever you save it — sharing it (e.g. via email attachment) is your own responsibility.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Internet — only for the two optional cases above (AI profile, photo search).
  • Camera — for photos you take of your stock; photos are stored locally only.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, boot receiver, vibration, wake lock — solely for local reminders about due care tasks; no data is transmitted for this.

Sharing with third parties

Other than the two cases described above, which you actively trigger yourself (Anthropic, Wikimedia), there is no sharing of data with third parties. No sale of data, no tracking, no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Your rights

Since all app data lives exclusively on your device, you're always in full control: view, change, and delete directly in the app or by uninstalling it. For questions about the two external services, contact the respective provider (Anthropic, Wikimedia) or us at the contact address above.
